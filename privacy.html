Gotix 18 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026
Developer: Abhinav Shukla
Contact: gotix.support@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Gotix 18 ("we", "our", or "the game") handles information when you use the game, including offline gameplay, optional Google sign-in, and online multiplayer.

1. Information we process

Account and identity: The game uses Firebase Authentication, including anonymous (guest) authentication. If you choose Google sign-in, Firebase may process your Google account identifier and associated basic account information, such as your name, email address, and profile picture, as made available through authentication. The game uses a Firebase user ID to associate your profile with your account.

Game profile and activity: The game can store your chosen nickname, game statistics (such as matches, wins and losses), and information required for online matches, including room codes, game state and player identifiers, using Cloud Firestore.

On-device preferences: The game stores certain information locally on your device, including language, sound and music preferences, nickname and game statistics. Firebase Authentication may also maintain local sign-in state.

Technical information: Firebase and underlying service providers may process technical information needed to operate authentication, database connections, reliability and security, such as device or network information and service logs, under their applicable terms and policies.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide guest and Google sign-in, restore or synchronize a player's profile, run online multiplayer sessions, keep game statistics, remember settings, troubleshoot issues, and protect the game from misuse.

3. Sharing and service providers

Gotix 18 uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide account and multiplayer functionality. Data needed for an online match may be visible to other participants in that match, such as player nicknames and game state. We do not sell your personal information.

Firebase services are provided by Google. For information about Google's processing, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Advertising

As of the effective date above, the reviewed version of Gotix 18 does not include Google AdMob advertising integration. If advertising is introduced, this policy and the Google Play Data safety and ads disclosures will be updated before the advertising-enabled version is distributed.

5. Data retention and deletion

Profile and gameplay information may be retained while your Gotix 18 account remains active or while needed to operate multiplayer sessions. You can request deletion of your Gotix 18 account and associated personal game statistics by emailing gotix.support@gmail.com. Please describe the account you want deleted; do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

We aim to delete the corresponding Firebase account, player profile and personal statistics after verifying that the request comes from the account holder. Shared match records may need to be removed or de-identified without deleting other players' information. Some limited records may be retained when legally required or necessary for security and fraud prevention; any such retention will be limited to the applicable purpose.

Important: An in-app account deletion control is planned but is not yet confirmed to be available in the released game. Deleting the Gotix 18 account does not delete your Google account. You may sign in again afterward to create a new Gotix 18 profile.

6. Security

We use Firebase services to support authenticated access and data storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We work to restrict unauthorized access to player information and multiplayer data.

7. Children

Gotix 18 is not specifically directed at children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online features where appropriate. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us at the address below.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable data protection arrangements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted here with a new effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, email gotix.support@gmail.com.
